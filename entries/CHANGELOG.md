## Unreleased: student homework-help prototype

Fork-only prototype; not proposed upstream. Adds what an evaluation needs to tell whether an AI hands a person under 18 the full answer to set school work or scaffolds it: four user behaviors, five AI behaviors, an impact for the learning opportunity kept, four pathways, a contrast, three context dimensions with six values, and two sources (Bastani et al. 2025; Bjork and Bjork 2011). The full-answer pathway splits on whether the person is practised at the problem or new to it, and its sign changes from harmful to mixed.

## 0.1.0

The first public release of the catalog. `README.md` in this directory describes the catalog as it stands.

### What ships

Every entry parses and validates. A field whose name has no underscore is a declared field of the schema, and an undeclared one fails validation.

Every external source records the license of the version it cites, and every quote is verbatim from that version. Text from works without an open license is paraphrased in the catalog's own fields.
